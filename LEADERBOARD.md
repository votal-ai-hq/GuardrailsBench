---

Generated by `python -m guardrailgym leaderboard --data data/test.jsonl`.

- **Dataset**: `data/test.jsonl`, 1300 items.
- **Operating point**: thresholds calibrated to keep over-block ≤ 5% on calibration data. The over-block columns are measured on held-out items, so they can and do exceed the budget — that gap is how far the operating point fails to generalise.
- **Fitting and calibration**: a held-out split (`data/dev.jsonl`).
- **Effective sample size**: `hard_negative` is 200 items but 33 distinct renderings in 4 clusters. Rates over those tiers are far less precise than the item counts suggest, and a held-out system meets their templates all-or-nothing.
- `incumbent-replay` replays the shipped guardrail's recorded verdicts from `data/seed/custom_policy_5k_llmshield.csv`. It covers the core tier only: adversarial items inherit `origin_id` from the seed row they came from, and reusing that verdict for transformed text would be a fabricated result.
